Why Train Jiu-Jitsu?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) is a martial art and combat sport based on ground fighting and submission holds. It focuses on the skill of taking an opponent to the ground, controlling one's opponent, and using techniques to force them to submit.

It enables a smaller, weaker person to successfully defend themself against a bigger, stronger opponent using leverage and technique.
